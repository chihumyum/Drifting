# Drifting trademark policy

The AGPL license grants copyright permissions for project-owned source code and
assets except where a file or notice identifies a separate license. It does not
grant trademark rights.

“Drifting”, “Drifting Writing Studio”, the official Drifting logo and app icon,
and official `drifting.app` / `drifting.cc` service identities are marks claimed
by the project maintainer. No claim of registration is made by this document.

You may use the word Drifting truthfully to identify this project, describe
compatibility, link to the original source, or state that your work is a fork.
You may not use the marks in a way that suggests sponsorship, endorsement, or
official status when none exists.

If you distribute a modified binary to others, use a distinct product name and
replace the logo, app icon, bundle identifier, URL/deep-link scheme, signing
identity, and official hosted-service endpoints unless you have written
permission. Keep the copyright and license notices required by AGPL.

This policy concerns source identification and consumer confusion. It does not
restrict running, modifying, or redistributing the AGPL-covered code, including
commercially.
