# Drifting 0.1.0-alpha.7

- Immersive writing: the stick of incense at the end of the paper's toolbar (or ⌘⇧↩) leaves only the paper. The paragraph you are writing stays in full ink, the rest fades, and Esc brings everything back as it was.
- Seven more interface languages: Deutsch, Español, Français, 日本語, 한국어, Português (Brasil) and 繁體中文. Pick one on first launch or in the account menu.
- Sign in with Google, and sign in from the account menu inside a book.
- The bookshelf has a left column with Import and Recently Deleted, a year of your writing days, and each book's last 30 days. Writing days follow your account.
- A new first launch, a short tour the first time you open a book, and empty views that say how to start.
- The element overview opens in a Relations layout. TODO & Materials shows comments beside the TODOs.
- Comments: the sticky-note rail is gone. The paper's comment button shows or hides the comment marks, new comments are written beside the selection, and pointing at a comment's mark lights its card.
- Larger interface text sizes, and every row of the account menu is one height.
- Images synced from another device now show their previews.

If you use Drifting on more than one Mac, update all of them: an older version pauses syncing a book once a newer one has written to it, and resumes after it is updated.

This is an early release. Keep independent copies of important manuscripts.
